# F08 — TIME / ORBITAL SEQUENCER

**Lineage / methods, NOT stylistic cloning:** Original Stage Casting 04 time-space instrument line; technical relation to Max, MIDI and spatial performance rather than copying artist-specific work.

**Research mechanism:** Multiple voiced clocks and spatial paths that can re-align; a sequencer that produces meaningful notes, rests and phrases.

**Signature instrument / free-tool route:** Orbital Hocket: three bounded phase oscillators, Euclidean-ish gates, MIDI note selector, quantized transition, OSC phase/state publisher. Tempo remains in one authoritative clock.

## Two ORIGINAL full-length composition briefs

### F08-1 — Orbital Hocket (128 BPM, target 4 min)
**Role:** dance / geometrical / playful

**Time-ordered form:** 0:00–0:34 one clock and one tone; 0:34–1:19 two voices exchange notes rather than overlap; 1:19–2:05 third clock adds long-range cycle; 2:05–2:46 clocks briefly coincide; 2:46–3:31 voice order inverts into a more physical groove; 3:31–4:00 unravel to initial clock.

**Distinct composition principle:** Hocket and voice role exchange, not randomized 16th sequence flood.

**Delay/reverb/mix design:** Tempo-related ping delays on two voice outputs, no global wet wash; clear front-to-back separation.

**Performable controls:** phase → musical time divergence; orbit → spatialization; BUTTON quantizes realignment at bar boundary.

**Visual spatial coupling:** three orbital paths share exact clock state and note event IDs.

**Output contract:** original 24bit/48k stereo preview; dry/wet and tonal/percussion stems; MIDI/score.json; synth/FX parameter snapshots; Show Score/control mapping; tested saved/reopenable DAW project where the local app allows; A/B listening annotations. No audio quality PASS without human audition.

### F08-2 — Phase Zero (150 BPM, target 4 min)
**Role:** climax / acceleration / release

**Time-ordered form:** 0:00–0:21 nearly empty synchrony; 0:21–1:03 progressively displaced accent layers; 1:03–1:52 long composite rhythm emerges; 1:52–2:26 remove strong grid to reveal hidden periodicity; 2:26–3:23 one successful realignment becomes the climax; 3:23–4:00 clocks reset at different speeds to silence.

**Distinct composition principle:** The peak is a structural realignment and a new rhythm, not just increased BPM or notes.

**Delay/reverb/mix design:** Very dry attacks and explicitly selected stereo echo points; short reverb only in the late afterpulse.

**Performable controls:** divergence → phase ratio bound; density → max event voices; FOOT realign; SAFE reset authoritative clock.

**Visual spatial coupling:** spatial orbit coincidence coincides with sonic phase alignment, not unrelated FFT reactivity.

**Output contract:** original 24bit/48k stereo preview; dry/wet and tonal/percussion stems; MIDI/score.json; synth/FX parameter snapshots; Show Score/control mapping; tested saved/reopenable DAW project where the local app allows; A/B listening annotations. No audio quality PASS without human audition.
