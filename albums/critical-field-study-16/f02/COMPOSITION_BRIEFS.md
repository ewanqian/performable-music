# F02 — RHYTHMIC MACHINES

**Lineage / methods, NOT stylistic cloning:** Byetone's Plastic Star / Death of a Typographer (2008), Symeta (2011), combined with user's original 26s Listening_02 observations. Develop original rhythmic identities.

**Research mechanism:** Elastic sub, percussion with identifiable transients, staggered call/response; silence is a groove device.

**Signature instrument / free-tool route:** Rubber Drum/Metal Transient: FM pitch sweep, short resonator, velocity-dependent timbre and transient/body separation; Surge XT + plugdata custom hit shaper.

## Two ORIGINAL full-length composition briefs

### F02-1 — Recoil (122 BPM, target 4 min)
**Role:** groove / dry / tactile

**Time-ordered form:** 0:00–0:31 percussive bass calls; 0:31–1:20 two complementary percussion replies; 1:20–1:55 remove kick and expose syncopation; 1:55–2:52 structural groove return with new answer; 2:52–3:35 phase-delayed metallic texture; 3:35–4:00 abrupt dry exit.

**Distinct composition principle:** Genuine call and response; one two-bar groove grows by replacing events, never by continuous clutter.

**Delay/reverb/mix design:** Short slap delay on metal only, hard dry transient front; sidechain/release tuned to bass notes and kick, no huge reverb.

**Performable controls:** bounce → pitch-envelope elastic tension; dialogue → call/answer balance; FOOT launches a deliberate breakdown.

**Visual spatial coupling:** impact geometry; original bass attacks are same events as short-lived volumetric deformations.

**Output contract:** original 24bit/48k stereo preview; dry/wet and tonal/percussion stems; MIDI/score.json; synth/FX parameter snapshots; Show Score/control mapping; tested saved/reopenable DAW project where the local app allows; A/B listening annotations. No audio quality PASS without human audition.

### F02-2 — Shear Force (138 BPM, target 4 min)
**Role:** industrial / peak / kinetic

**Time-ordered form:** 0:00–0:47 sub-only tension; 0:47–1:28 4+3 step asymmetry; 1:28–2:12 fractured kick and one exposed midrange signature; 2:12–2:53 complete rhythmic subtraction; 2:53–3:33 one decisive transformation and deep impact; 3:33–4:00 heavy, slow deceleration.

**Distinct composition principle:** Architectural rupture, nonstandard phrase grouping, and earned contrast; not a louder Recoil.

**Delay/reverb/mix design:** Parallel distorted midrange bus with dry sub preserved, short metallic space, 1–2 event-locked long tails.

**Performable controls:** shear → clock disagreement; impact → bounded bass/timbre pressure; FOOT triggers rupture.

**Visual spatial coupling:** a rigid structure shears and reforms on the same override event.

**Output contract:** original 24bit/48k stereo preview; dry/wet and tonal/percussion stems; MIDI/score.json; synth/FX parameter snapshots; Show Score/control mapping; tested saved/reopenable DAW project where the local app allows; A/B listening annotations. No audio quality PASS without human audition.
