# F05 — FOUND-SOUND / CORPUS INSTRUMENT

**Lineage / methods, NOT stylistic cloning:** Amon Tobin early Bricolage (1997), Permutation (1998), Supermodified (2000), Foley Room (2007), ISAM (2011): source transformation from recordings to playable instruments. Record your own sources.

**Research mechanism:** A small personal corpus of physically interesting original audio, segmented and played as an expressive instrument, not a sample pack montage.

**Signature instrument / free-tool route:** Object Corpus: 20 self-recorded object/material hits; segmentation/transient detection; descriptors or manual metadata; mapping pitch, attack/decay, resonance and articulation. Optional FluCoMa if available and licensed.

## Two ORIGINAL full-length composition briefs

### F05-1 — Contact Archive (110 BPM, target 4 min)
**Role:** intimate / curious / object-sound

**Time-ordered form:** 0:00–0:35 three original material contacts; 0:35–1:17 learn their articulation; 1:17–2:12 assign them a rhythmic dialogue; 2:12–2:54 stretch one into a quasi-melodic sustain; 2:54–3:28 return hits against the sustain; 3:28–4:00 end on an unprocessed object sound.

**Distinct composition principle:** Composed identity and original sound provenance, no generic drum rack substitutions.

**Delay/reverb/mix design:** Dry percussive foreground, one predelay/short resonant chamber on selected contacts, spatial motion meaningful to materials.

**Performable controls:** material → corpus cluster; articulation → strike/scrape/resonate; PINCH commits a phrase.

**Visual spatial coupling:** materials represented as simple spatial traces, not literal photos.

**Output contract:** original 24bit/48k stereo preview; dry/wet and tonal/percussion stems; MIDI/score.json; synth/FX parameter snapshots; Show Score/control mapping; tested saved/reopenable DAW project where the local app allows; A/B listening annotations. No audio quality PASS without human audition.

### F05-2 — Object Swarm (142 BPM, target 4 min)
**Role:** club / dense / percussive

**Time-ordered form:** 0:00–0:25 isolate one mechanical contact; 0:25–1:06 derive a kick/bass from contact; 1:06–1:54 accelerating hocket of different materials; 1:54–2:22 tempo persists through sudden empty field; 2:22–3:20 multi-object groove fractures and resolves; 3:20–4:00 source solo becomes percussion again.

**Distinct composition principle:** One corpus as tightly produced dance/industrial system; not simply more of Contact Archive.

**Delay/reverb/mix design:** Per-material parallel distortion and envelope shaping; sub kept centered, bright shards with short spatial return.

**Performable controls:** swarm → voice count with max polyphony; material_bias → selection distribution; FOOT mutes all but main pulse.

**Visual spatial coupling:** number of spatial bodies corresponds to playing object-voices, event-synchronized.

**Output contract:** original 24bit/48k stereo preview; dry/wet and tonal/percussion stems; MIDI/score.json; synth/FX parameter snapshots; Show Score/control mapping; tested saved/reopenable DAW project where the local app allows; A/B listening annotations. No audio quality PASS without human audition.

## Studio acceptance
- Listen to both without visuals at matched playback loudness; they must sound like different compositions, not different effect presets.
- At least one identifiable musical event, one meaningful structural contrast and one intentional silence in each piece.
- Distortion, delay, reverb, stereo/space and sub-bass must be purposeful and bounded; document release/tail design.
- No artist recordings, copied melody, cloned sound signatures, unlicensed code, private WAV or unverified stage claims.
