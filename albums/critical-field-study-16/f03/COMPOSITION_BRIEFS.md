# F03 — SPATIAL / SPECTRAL ARCHITECTURE

**Lineage / methods, NOT stylistic cloning:** Ryoichi Kurokawa early Copynature/READ to rheo; scene is conceived as sound+image from the outset. Not a copy of his datasets or shots.

**Research mechanism:** One multidimensional spatial field with source location and timbral space sharing underlying coordinate/state.

**Signature instrument / free-tool route:** Spectral Mesh: controlled bank of resonators fed by own field recordings + moving grains; plugdata biquad bank or SC Klank-like approach; optional real 4ch bus after rider verification.

## Two ORIGINAL full-length composition briefs

### F03-1 — Volumetric Weather (92 BPM, target 4 min)
**Role:** ambient / fluid / immersive

**Time-ordered form:** 0:00–0:56 one captured environmental grain; 0:56–1:42 timbral altitude shift; 1:42–2:18 wide stillness; 2:18–3:11 weather front/pitched resonance moves through field; 3:11–4:00 evaporate to source recording.

**Distinct composition principle:** Mostly texture with genuinely evolving spectral form; no need for busy click track.

**Delay/reverb/mix design:** Three depth planes: near dry, medium early reflection, far late reverb; automate decay only during designed spatial transitions.

**Performable controls:** altitude → resonant center; weather → cloud density; position → speaker/stereo panning; manual gesture hold.

**Visual spatial coupling:** a spatial volume with a legible near/far relationship. Stereo fallback uses meaningful depth, not fake verified surround.

**Output contract:** original 24bit/48k stereo preview; dry/wet and tonal/percussion stems; MIDI/score.json; synth/FX parameter snapshots; Show Score/control mapping; tested saved/reopenable DAW project where the local app allows; A/B listening annotations. No audio quality PASS without human audition.

### F03-2 — Tectonic Glass (120 BPM, target 4 min)
**Role:** impact / spectral rupture

**Time-ordered form:** 0:00–0:25 weak brittle contact; 0:25–1:14 resonance stabilizes into 3-note harmonic cell; 1:14–2:04 deformation causes partial frequency splits; 2:04–2:42 stop all high content; 2:42–3:24 one collapse / reconstitution; 3:24–4:00 small fragments fall away.

**Distinct composition principle:** Granular/spectral transformations form a time sculpture; structural impact without generic cinematic riser.

**Delay/reverb/mix design:** Multiband control for brittle highs; frequency-dependent room tails and pre-delay to preserve attacks.

**Performable controls:** fracture → count of resonant shards; mass → low/medium harmonic weighting; PINCH capture.

**Visual spatial coupling:** same fractured resonator graph also determines point-cloud connectivity.

**Output contract:** original 24bit/48k stereo preview; dry/wet and tonal/percussion stems; MIDI/score.json; synth/FX parameter snapshots; Show Score/control mapping; tested saved/reopenable DAW project where the local app allows; A/B listening annotations. No audio quality PASS without human audition.

- Distortion, delay, reverb, stereo/space and sub-bass must be purposeful and bounded; document release/tail design.
