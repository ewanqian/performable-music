# F04 — COPY / RESYNTHESIS / MEMORY

**Lineage / methods, NOT stylistic cloning:** Alva Noto's early Transform (2001) and Xerrox Vol.1 (2007): digital copying/misreading can be compositional material. Use only original input.

**Research mechanism:** Original source repeatedly transformed into structurally meaningful descendants, with a reversible lineage of sonic identity.

**Signature instrument / free-tool route:** Copy Engine: local audio buffer, lossy/bit-depth variants, granular offset, spectral freeze, bounded feedback, preset snapshots; commit derived versions to disk for repeatability.

## Two ORIGINAL full-length composition briefs

### F04-1 — First Copy (96 BPM, target 4 min)
**Role:** minimal / uncanny / patient

**Time-ordered form:** 0:00–0:50 expose one original contact/room sound; 0:50–1:42 one degree of degradation; 1:42–2:21 derived pitch becomes a counterline; 2:21–3:09 material turns spatial and distant; 3:09–4:00 near-perfect return with one missing detail.

**Distinct composition principle:** Source remains audible until the final transformation; it is a piece about remembering sound.

**Delay/reverb/mix design:** Convolution or early reflections tied to source, not constant wet pad. Two tempo-free echo distances separated by register.

**Performable controls:** copy_depth → destructive lineage stage; memory → how many prior generations can answer; BUTTON commits a generation.

**Visual spatial coupling:** source shape duplicates and misregisters; track generation IDs in shared score.

**Output contract:** original 24bit/48k stereo preview; dry/wet and tonal/percussion stems; MIDI/score.json; synth/FX parameter snapshots; Show Score/control mapping; tested saved/reopenable DAW project where the local app allows; A/B listening annotations. No audio quality PASS without human audition.

### F04-2 — Xerographic Fault (134 BPM, target 4 min)
**Role:** aggressive / error / discontinuous

**Time-ordered form:** 0:00–0:40 original loop becomes a machine; 0:40–1:18 controlled copy errors form groove; 1:18–2:06 fast switching between generations; 2:06–2:36 rupture/dropped frame silence; 2:36–3:27 hard reassembled drum machine; 3:27–4:00 source unexpectedly returns clean.

**Distinct composition principle:** Rhythmic corruption rather than dreamy copying, with a genuinely new composition from intermediate generations.

**Delay/reverb/mix design:** Sample-rate reduction at selected transitions, transient-preserving delay for 2/4 events, low wet on sustained sources.

**Performable controls:** corrupt → bit/segment transform index; repeat → re-insertion probability; FOOT restore original.

**Visual spatial coupling:** temporal discontinuity is audible and visible with same generation markers.

**Output contract:** original 24bit/48k stereo preview; dry/wet and tonal/percussion stems; MIDI/score.json; synth/FX parameter snapshots; Show Score/control mapping; tested saved/reopenable DAW project where the local app allows; A/B listening annotations. No audio quality PASS without human audition.

## Studio acceptance
- Listen to both without visuals at matched playback loudness; they must sound like different compositions, not different effect presets.
- At least one identifiable musical event, one meaningful structural contrast and one intentional silence in each piece.
- Distortion, delay, reverb, stereo/space and sub-bass must be purposeful and bounded; document release/tail design.
- No artist recordings, copied melody, cloned sound signatures, unlicensed code, private WAV or unverified stage claims.
