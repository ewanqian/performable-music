# F07 — EMBODIED / INTERACTION-COMPOSED

**Lineage / methods, NOT stylistic cloning:** Daito Manabe's 2008 myoelectric/face instrument research plus later algorithmic and agent systems. Vision Pro DJ public video is evidence of a demonstration, not open technical implementation.

**Research mechanism:** The performer changes a musical system's rules, not 50 raw synth knobs. Work first with desktop/MIDI replay before real AVP and never imply actual EMG capture.

**Signature instrument / free-tool route:** Gesture Voltage: dual state engines; stable glide, event hysteresis, hand zone control; self-contained OSC MIDI fallback; spectral/timbral pressure is bounded and neutral-safe.

## Two ORIGINAL full-length composition briefs

### F07-1 — Virtual Muscle (126 BPM, target 4 min)
**Role:** gesture / elastic / tense

**Time-ordered form:** 0:00–0:33 dry repeated motor phrase; 0:33–1:16 a virtual effort envelope changes harmonic excitation; 1:16–2:08 voice modulations interact; 2:08–2:50 controlled failure / loss of tracking changes nothing unsafe; 2:50–3:30 recover with new melodic contour; 3:30–4:00 fade effort, leave source.

**Distinct composition principle:** Movement articulation and instrument response are compositional; gesture dynamics are recorded/replayable, not random automation.

**Delay/reverb/mix design:** Bounded pressure→saturation with dry bypass, transient-safe multiband envelope, stereo movement never on master gain.

**Performable controls:** effort (simulated/derived) → pressure; pinch edge→event; dwell→hold; FOOT→recover/kill.

**Visual spatial coupling:** show both human control and system state, using hand skeleton only if actual tracking is available.

**Output contract:** original 24bit/48k stereo preview; dry/wet and tonal/percussion stems; MIDI/score.json; synth/FX parameter snapshots; Show Score/control mapping; tested saved/reopenable DAW project where the local app allows; A/B listening annotations. No audio quality PASS without human audition.

### F07-2 — Override Gesture (132 BPM, target 4 min)
**Role:** peak / performative / dramatic

**Time-ordered form:** 0:00–0:41 one audible decision changes rhythm; 0:41–1:32 player locks an unstable beat; 1:32–2:14 tension grows with declining event density; 2:14–2:40 deliberate long near-silence; 2:40–3:25 one foot override creates decisive sonic/spatial change; 3:25–4:00 performer chooses deconstruction.

**Distinct composition principle:** Single performable moment, strong agency and peak contrast; not just more modulated sounds.

**Delay/reverb/mix design:** High-impact short transient plus long, carefully bounded spectral tail; dry sub and transient peak checked; manual hard mute independent of network.

**Performable controls:** FOOT OVERRIDE is priority; left/right gestures shape pre/post-event state. tracking stale→neutral.

**Visual spatial coupling:** same event.id deforms both speaker-scene and large spatial geometry; flash rules controlled for audience safety.

**Output contract:** original 24bit/48k stereo preview; dry/wet and tonal/percussion stems; MIDI/score.json; synth/FX parameter snapshots; Show Score/control mapping; tested saved/reopenable DAW project where the local app allows; A/B listening annotations. No audio quality PASS without human audition.

## Studio acceptance
- Listen to both without visuals at matched playback loudness; they must sound like different compositions, not different effect presets.
- At least one identifiable musical event, one meaningful structural contrast and one intentional silence in each piece.
- Distortion, delay, reverb, stereo/space and sub-bass must be purposeful and bounded; document release/tail design.
- No artist recordings, copied melody, cloned sound signatures, unlicensed code, private WAV or unverified stage claims.
