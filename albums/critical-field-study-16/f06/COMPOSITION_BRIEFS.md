## Two ORIGINAL full-length composition briefs

### F06-1 — Gravity Phrase (84 BPM, target 4 min)
**Role:** emotional / cinematic but not game-like

**Time-ordered form:** 0:00–0:55 strong 2-bar phrase alone with expressive dynamics; 0:55–1:34 counterline changes cadence; 1:34–2:23 low spectral shadow answers harmony; 2:23–3:06 remove most notes, slow harmonic transformation; 3:06–4:00 original motif returns in changed register/chord then stops.

**Distinct composition principle:** Narrative driven by original harmonic tension and real phrasing, not repeating high notes with stock strings.

**Delay/reverb/mix design:** Dry piano anchors center; spectral resonance creates one 'shadow' role; short and long space buses contrasted at returns.

**Performable controls:** memory → delayed phrase history; harmonic_weight → note/chord voicing density; foot cue instead of arbitrary note control.

**Visual spatial coupling:** motif traces appear later as delayed paths; scene changes on true harmonic arrivals.

**Output contract:** original 24bit/48k stereo preview; dry/wet and tonal/percussion stems; MIDI/score.json; synth/FX parameter snapshots; Show Score/control mapping; tested saved/reopenable DAW project where the local app allows; A/B listening annotations. No audio quality PASS without human audition.

### F06-2 — Luminous Afterimage (108 BPM, target 4 min)
**Role:** release / melodic / warm

**Time-ordered form:** 0:00–0:39 incomplete motif; 0:39–1:20 clear answered phrase; 1:20–2:02 rhythm enters with carefully staggered counterline; 2:02–2:45 remove bass and make harmony weightless; 2:45–3:29 delayed phrase returns in altered harmony; 3:29–4:00 final release with the original melodic material and limited tail.

**Distinct composition principle:** A brighter narrative outcome, with distinct chords, gesture of arrival and remembered theme; not merely Gravity Phrase transposed.

**Delay/reverb/mix design:** Avoid global hall reverb; keep intimate leading attacks and create tails only on phrase endings. Stereo image should move slowly.

**Performable controls:** phrase_release → orchestrated scene change; color → harmonic/filtered counterpart; optional small gestural rubato bounded by tempo clock.

**Visual spatial coupling:** from restricted lattice to spacious field; calm release after the stage peak.
