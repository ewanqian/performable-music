# Week 01 Scoreboard — Nov 3–9, 2026

The goal is not to complete four polished tracks. The goal is to hear four different mechanisms clearly enough to keep only two.

## Rules

- Maximum first-pass build time per exercise: 90 minutes.
- Every exercise must leave an audio artifact.
- Do not add a new plugin/tool unless the exercise is blocked without it.
- Do not keep a study because it was difficult to make.
- By Day 7, reject at least two studies.

## Week 01 pass condition

PASS when:
- four audible studies exist;
- at least two are rejected with reasons;
- two surviving mechanisms have a clear next experiment;
- you can describe each survivor without saying only “it sounds like KASHIWA / Daito / YMO / CAPSULE”.
