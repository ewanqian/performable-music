## Two legitimate but different forms

**Club / DJ-oriented form:** sustained bodily groove and phrase-level anticipation; 8/16/32-bar cues, a usable entrance/exit, repeated identity with meaningful variations, a clear control of low-end and transition. A common *optional* 4/4 template is 16 intro / 32 groove / 32 drive / 16 brief breakdown / 32 return / 32 variation / 16 outro at 128 BPM (~5m30). Do **not** paste this form onto all 48 directions. See Dennis DeSantis, *Making Music*: https://cdn-resources.ableton.com/resources/uploads/makingmusic/MakingMusic_DennisDeSantis.pdf

**Artistic AV form:** an original sensory, social or technical proposition changes the relationship between musical events and what is seen or physically performed. Narrative and event control can be independent of DJ phrasing. But an AV-specific concept is **not** permission for bad audio, random silence or unstructured tracks.

**Hybrid target:** a compelling club pulse is the **body**, a legible original premise is the **mind**; music and visual share curated events, not automatic FFT amplitude mapping. The music must work without picture; the artwork must change the meaning of the performance beyond being a visualization.

## Quality distinction / one-lane metrics

| Lens | Must be heard or observed | Failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Groove | physical continuity, kick/bass interlock, swung/groovy ghost notes or compelling other rhythmic anchor | random note probability or missing beatbed |
| Sonic identity | one original timbral signature, source gesture or recognizable motif | factory patch parade |
| Form | a musical question, development, counterphrase and earned return | arbitrary 30s snippets and unexplained dead gaps |
| Tension | suspense from delayed answers, shift of meter/space/timbre, controlled escalation and release | only louder layers, increasing hats or white-noise riser |
| Performance | one human decision audibly changes the music at a safe phrase boundary | merely controls a shader or volume |
| AV concept | one causally defensible relation between sonic and spatial worlds | decorative spectrum effect as the idea |
| Engineering | saved/reopened DAW, real audible WAV, aligned stems, repeatable playback, fallback | code/metrics only or misrepresented render |

## Non-negotiable anti-fragmentation rules

- An initial generated 20–35 second fragment is **not** automatically a complete piece or valid stage candidate. If the concept cannot sustain at least two full musical phrase cycles, reject it.
- A serious producer does not add three or four layers merely to manufacture excitement; the new layer must change the musical role, answer or texture.
- Keep drums/bass a continuous baseline for club candidates. Purposeful short beatless passages must preserve pulse by another audible mechanism; exceptional full stop must be narratively earned and rehearsed.
- Do not use one master volume or loudness rise as the sole demonstration of development.
- No second soundtrack for the visuals before the first version earns an audio-only pass.
