## Four directions to audition, not artist clones

## Nonnegotiable sequence

1. Inventory existing actual WAV/MIDI/render outputs. Do not regenerate just because source is present.
2. **First listen to 006 A/B** as neutral, level-matched, audio-only exports. Restore verified editable DAW version if available.
3. Choose exactly one **original 2-bar bass/percussion idea** to iterate. Render **two structurally different** 16-bar versions, not a volume-only comparison.
4. Compare groove, sound identity, phrasing, contrast and desire to replay **without visuals**. Record timestamped human feedback and one explicit cut.
5. Select at most three short candidates from existing/modified material. Produce a single 60–90s polished segment with aligned `KICK/PERC/BASS/ATMOS` stems or equivalent recorded roles.
6. Only then attach visual/lighting events and audition audio alone again before stage promotion.

## Six-dimension human gate

Score 1–5: memorable musical material (20%), form (20%), distinctive timbre (20%), critical listening appeal (20%), performance decisions (10%), engineering reliability (10%). Human listener scores—not agent self-scores—decide. Any of first four below 3 → revise; show candidate requires first four >=4 plus actual audible evidence.

## Output contract

`audition/<run-id>/` (local/ignored if rights unclear):
- `A.wav`, `B.wav`, explicit sample rate/bit depth and level-matching method
- `stems/` and `composition-plan.json` or MIDI / editable DAW session
- `blind-listening.csv`: names concealed until judgement, timestamp and human notes
- `QA.md`: installed DAW/plugin versions, actual save/reopen/export status, technical measurements and failures
- one approved next action; no automatically promoted tracks

## Musical → visual contract

Known MIDI/Show Score events specify actual kick/snare/drop, foot decision and act. LinkOSC `/pattack` and `/pvol` add live signal analysis; they **cannot** guarantee exact drum identity. The external display should never compensate for weak audio. Keep original composition master and a stereo fallback.
