## Specific questions for 20-minute guided listening

For each work, write:
1. Exactly which events are heard in the first 30 seconds?
2. What repeats, and when does repeating become a new musical function?
3. What is **not present**? Silence, dry transient, absent kick, missing bass, cutoff reverb.
4. Which sound physically occupies near/mid/far distance? Which production decisions make that happen?
5. What transition is earned by the preceding tension, not merely by a loud hit?
6. What would still be interesting if the accompanying visual were switched off?
7. What is a legitimate original mechanism to test without copying material?

## Mapping to the user's portfolio

- **TIMER:** research experience in temporal visual structures; supports F01 precision and F08 sequencer.
- **Drop Flow:** a visual/system pathway from a single event to a changing space; supports F03 spectral architecture and F04 memory/copy. Verify credits before describing external collaborations.
- **Stage Casting / Entropy / REACTOR:** performance control, multichannel image and cueing knowledge; supports F07 body-control and the shared Show Score system.
- These are **analytical connections**, not claims that the previous works already contained today's sound engines.

## Non-copying & review

Facts belong with a linked source. Analysis is a hypothesis. New composition, synthesis code, MIDI, original recordings and visuals must be independently created or properly licensed. No exact artist samples/recognized melodies/visual sequences or unlicensed source. Do not claim you have reproduced an un-released Vision Pro DJ setup from public footage.
