# Lineages — Working Map

This file separates **documented influence** from **analytical genealogy**.

## KASHIWA Daisuke

### Documented / strongly sourced

- Early band background: guitarist/composer in Yodaka, associated with post-rock.
- Early solo work was described as combining classical material, electronica, environmental/field-recorded sound and dense editing.
- KASHIWA has named **Queen**, especially _Queen II_, as a major musical influence.
- He has also named Italian noise artist **M.B. / Maurizio Bianchi** as an important influence.
- In the _program music II_ period he said that outside work he listened to almost nothing but **jazz**, which influenced his desire to use double bass; piano remained a central instrument.
- _program music I_ explicitly used literary stories as compositional motifs, connecting the work to a programmatic/narrative approach to form.

### Analytical genealogy to test, not assert as direct influence

```text
19th-century programmatic / narrative form
            +
long-form post-rock accumulation and release
            +
2000s electronica / glitch / detailed editing
            +
piano / chamber / post-classical vocabulary
            +
jazz instrumentation and phrasing
            +
noise / environmental sound as structural material
            ↓
KASHIWA-type long-form hybrid composition
            ↓
TITAN: simpler tonal elements + piano + powerful electronics + bass-led scale
```

The point is not to assign a single genre. It is to study how narrative form, editing, acoustic instruments and electronic mass are coordinated.

## Daito Manabe

### Documented / strongly sourced

- Grew up in a musical family and was immersed in music and programming.
- Experience as a **DJ** and **jazz-band member**.
- 90s **hip-hop** is documented as an important part of his DJ formation; official/archival material also describes strong hip-hop roots.
- Encounter with **Iannis Xenakis** inspired research into mathematical approaches to music generation.
- DJ practice spans hip-hop, IDM, juke, halftime, trap, kwaito and related club forms.
- His 2026 _dm studies 000–004_ explicitly starts from the physicality and structure of club music, combining mathematical principles with intricate sound design.
- His wider artistic practice treats body, data, programming, audiovisual media and feedback systems as compositional material.

### Analytical genealogy

```text
jazz / DJ practice / 90s hip-hop
          +
Xenakis / mathematical music generation
          +
IDM / juke / footwork / club structure
          +
programming / sensor systems / audiovisual feedback
          +
custom music software
          ↓
process-based performable electronic systems
```

## Why both matter here

They are useful precisely because they are not the same school.

- **KASHIWA axis:** narrative / melody / piano / emotional long-form / detailed edit.
- **Manabe axis:** process / mathematics / club physicality / computation / sensing / system behavior.

A possible Performable Music position is the intersection:

> emotionally legible long-form music whose structure is generated and performed through explicit computational and spatial systems.
