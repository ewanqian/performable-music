**Source evaluation:** FACT = sourced description of external practice, INFERENCE = why it might matter to this show, EXPERIMENT = our authored mechanism. Do not promote stage photos or fan accounts to confirmed technical facts. Recheck links, licenses and versions when implementing.

## One listening record per artist / work

1. Work/recording metadata, year, recording rights and link.
2. Timestamp 00:00, first 8/16/32 bars; identify groove anchor and sound identity.
3. Record the most significant *musically motivated* change and what prepared it.
4. Mark a transition that could have been removed without harming the music.
5. Specify the exactly ONE compositional operation worth rebuilding originally.
6. Separate DJ-friendliness / emotional continuity / stage AV specificity.
7. No audio files or transcribed protected musical phrases committed to public Git.

## What not to do

Do not ask an Agent to make a track "in the style of" the named artist. Do not impose the same 16-bar drop formula on every genre or confuse 48 mechanisms with 48 finished tracks.
