## Rules

- Research may be copied as bibliographic links and original analysis; not copyrighted recordings, exact patterns, artists' samples, or protected visual software.
- **Music-only gate** is mandatory. A clever Max/Metal/AI workflow does not mean a strong track.

## Key research conclusion

The repeatable unit is **not a style tag**. It is a *musical decision with evidence*:
`musical question → original material → groove anchor → transformation → expected answer / surprise → performance action → audio-only acceptance`.

A productive Agent should be able to tell which previous musical function is preserved, what changed, whether the listener can follow it, and which candidate was rejected. Complexity of sequencing, number of generated WAV files and DSP/model prestige are not musical-success measures.

## Seven families of mechanisms worth building as software services

1. **Rhythmic anchor generator** — generates kick/bass/ghost-note relationships; must support controlled humanization, no all-track random timing.
2. **Sound identity lab** — create original single instrument with playable multi-state timbral evolution; produce dry source and altered variants.
3. **Transition composer** — 8/16/32-bar musically earned question/answer, anticipation and release; explicit reasons for silence.
4. **Live-take recorder** — curated controlled improvisation, capture plus retrospective editing, not arbitrary unlogged randomness.
5. **Corpus instrument** — legally owned or recorded source objects, onset segmentation, timbre grouping and retrieval.
6. **Performance score router** — phrase-quantized decisions, source-of-truth DAW/Show Score and bounded controls.
7. **Independent listening QA** — hidden labels, level-matched audio, timestamp critique, acceptance/reject and a modified next hypothesis.
