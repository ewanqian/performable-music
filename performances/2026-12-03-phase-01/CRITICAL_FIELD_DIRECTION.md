# CRITICAL FIELD / 临界场 — Phase 01

**Target:** 2026-12-03 · a coherent personally performed audiovisual album / live set.
**Status:** creative hypothesis + original local 78.75-second audio prototype. No verified venue rider, stage integration, Max patch, or critical listening acceptance.

## Thesis

One person plays a field of sound, light and space. Through bodily input the system acquires structure and pressure, becomes unstable, and appears to resist control. The performer must retake control. The goal is **musical presence and discernible agency**, not "AI generated music" as a selling point.

## Four acts, 16 min target

| Act | Minutes | Working title | Music | Performer / visual logic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | 00–03 | INITIAL CONDITION | scarce events, low tone, an original motif | hand reveals a spatial field out of near-darkness |
| II | 03–07 | FIELD | multiple clocks, angular metallic percussion and active low end | hand motion controls divergence and spatial geometry |
| III | 07–12 | OVERRIDE | negative space -> large bass/structure event -> unstable but controlled rhythm | hold suspends, foot launches decisive musical/visual event |
| IV | 12–16 | AFTERIMAGE | original motif returns transformed, expressive release | gesture/input is removed, musical memory survives |

Fallback: an excellent 12-minute piece is better than an under-rehearsed 20-minute set.

## One signature scene to finish before expanding the show

Make the 31.9–45s suspension lead to the 45s OVERRIDE: remove nearly all energy, narrow the field, hold performer motion; foot trigger releases a synchronised audio/visual expansion. Stage field responds to the same event and semantic state. Prefer spatial scale and contrast over repeated harsh flashing.

## Actual production architecture

1. **Composer**: fix melody/rhythm/tension before adding effects.
2. **Audio engine**: four stems first; one signature FM/spectral/granular layer later. No paid plugin required (SuperCollider and other free tools are valid).
3. **Live control**: structured Show Score with ENERGY / PRESSURE / SPACE / FRACTURE, scene and event IDs.
4. **Visual engine**: a shared spatial world with deliberate camera/field transitions; Jitter/WebGL, no generic FFT bars.
5. **Reliability**: stereo first. Quad/spatial only if venue and hardware justify. Physical KILL not dependent on browser/headset/network.
6. **Evidence**: real A/B listening, offline render, local device test and show rehearsal must precede claims of quality.

## Human critical listening test

1. Does the OVERRIDE impact feel earned by the silence before it?
2. Is there a recognizable musical idea beyond "sound design is cool"?
3. Is a real performance choice perceptible?
4. Which part would you keep if the visuals were blacked out?

If any answer is weak, revise composition before expanding stages. Use the [Expert Production Protocol](../../research/agent-music/EXPERT_PRODUCTION_PROTOCOL.md) rather than generating more generic tracks.
