## Concept

Performer hand gestures or explicit musical cues drive an **imagined neural response** to original music. The simulated field can influence a controlled future arrangement choice. It is an artwork about agency, listening and feedback—not a tool that infers real EEG from audio.

The December 3 event is described as brain-signal-related. Unless a source-qualified EEG feed is actually provided, label every on-screen signal as **SYNTHETIC / ARTISTIC SIMULATION**; a neurological term is not itself measured evidence.

## Shared state, not decorative FFT

`music-event-id` / `session_id + seq` is the join key between performance sound, light and 3D. DAW produces reliable exact drum events; LinkOSC adds percussive/spectral analytics, not fictitious drum identities. A neural animation is not measured neural firing.
