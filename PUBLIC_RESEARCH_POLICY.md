# Public Research Policy

This repository is public. Treat publication as an intentional act, not a dump of working memory.

## Do not publish

- copyrighted reference audio, stems, ripped media, or full score transcriptions;
- private chats, emails, contact details, credentials, tokens, account IDs;
- personal financial/medical/private-life information;
- confidential client material;
- third-party source code without a verified license permitting reuse.

## Publishable by default

- bibliographic metadata and source links;
- timestamped listening annotations;
- derived audio descriptors and feature tables;
- original diagrams, MIDI, patches, code and recordings;
- research questions, method notes and experiment logs;
- short quotations where legally appropriate, with attribution;
- summaries and analytical claims clearly labeled as interpretation.

## Three evidence labels

Use one of these in research notes when ambiguity matters:

- **FACT** — directly supported by a cited source.
- **INFERENCE** — our interpretation of the work or lineage.
- **EXPERIMENT** — an original practice-based reconstruction or test.

## Reference works

A nonprofit or educational intention does not automatically authorize copying music, recordings, artwork, scores or source code. Keep reference audio outside Git. Use legally obtained copies locally for analysis, and publish only the minimum derived data needed to make the research reproducible.

## Artist respect

Studying an artist does not imply endorsement, collaboration or authorship by that artist. Write “inspired by”, “method reference”, or “analytical lineage” instead of implying direct affiliation.

## Third-party software

Record project name, URL, author/organization, version/date checked, license, and exactly how it is used in `references/ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS.md`.
